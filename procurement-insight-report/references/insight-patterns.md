# Insight Patterns

Use these patterns to interpret procurement facts without drifting away from the source of truth.

## Good insight questions

- Is quote volume rising or falling?
- Which status bucket dominates the period?
- Is there an unusual share of on-going or unknown items?
- Are cancellations clustering around stock, timing, or client response?
- Which assignees appear overloaded?
- Which customers or channels recur in stalled work?
- Is turnaround getting faster or slower?

## Good insight structure

1. State the factual pattern.
2. Explain the likely operational meaning.
3. Suggest a next action.

Example:

- Fact: A high share of current-week records remain `On going`.
- Insight: Work may be entering the pipeline faster than it is being converted or closed.
- Action: Review aging and assignee distribution before backlog compounds.

## Boundaries

Do not:

- claim a root cause that the fields do not support
- turn one noisy record into a broad business narrative
- ignore confidence or endpoint conflicts

## Useful action framing

- follow up
- investigate
- rebalance
- escalate
- monitor
- standardize
